## Scope and mode

Whole site (Home, Work, Playwright case study, About, Contact). Mode: Persuade. Recruiters must, within one screen, know who Sushanta is, what he automates, and how to reach him or get the résumé.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site runs like a CI/CD pipeline run. Each page is a stage, each measured result is a passed cell, and the run ends waiting on the recruiter's approval. Refuses the default dev-portfolio of centred hero, skills grid and project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night navy (#0B1B2E) hero bands over a long-exposure motorway photo; light blue-grey (#EEF2F6) body; one signal green for passed states, primary buttons and result cells (green tint, dark green text). Bold grotesk (Schibsted Grotesk) for display and body; JetBrains Mono only for stage labels, statuses and tool tags. Square-ish 6px corners, hairline rules, tables instead of cards.

STORY: The visitor sees a pipeline of his career, reads three roles as a stage view with green result cells, sees the wealth-platform specialism, and acts: download résumé or email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width navy hero over the road photo. Left: headline "I test the systems that move other people's money." at ~5.5rem, sub, green "Download resume" and "See the results" buttons. Bottom of hero: the five-stage pipeline bar spanning the width, ending on Contact "Waiting on you".

FORM: Brief-pinned (figma-design-notes.md, "v2 – pipeline"). seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: a green signal travels the photographed road and hands off to the pipeline bar, whose stages flip to passed in 180ms steps.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
