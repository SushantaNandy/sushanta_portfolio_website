# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, with Three.js for the 3D world.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Sushanta Nandy for a full-time SDET / QA automation role, especially at wealth and asset management firms. They skim quickly, often between other candidates, and need to judge skill and fit in minutes.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site that gets Sushanta Nandy hired as an SDET / QA automation engineer. Success: a recruiter understands what Sushanta automates, sees measured results, and makes contact or downloads the résumé.

## Positioning

ISTQB-certified SDET (nearly 5 years, since Jan 2022) specialising in wealth and asset management platforms: trading, portfolios, fees and charges, onboarding, HNI models. Migrates legacy suites to Playwright and builds AI-assisted test tooling. The site itself is framed as a CI/CD pipeline run that ends waiting for the recruiter's approval.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site built with Astro.
- Main experience: a full explorable 3D world (decided 2026-11-10, inspired by bruno-simon.com but not copying it).
- Plain version: Home, Work, one case study (Playwright migration), About, Contact, built from the Figma design, reachable from every screen and used automatically for reduced motion or no WebGL. Design and decisions: `figma-design-notes.md`.
- Contact: email with copy button, résumé download, LinkedIn, GitHub. No phone number on the site.
- Undecided: hosting/deploy target.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over claims: every skill shown is backed by a real project, link, or code.
2. Fast to scan: a recruiter gets who, what, and how to contact within the first screen, even while the 3D world loads, and can always skip to the plain version.
3. The site itself is evidence of web craft: fast, accessible, and well built.
